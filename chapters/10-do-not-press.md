::: warn
**Do not press any button on this page.**

These buttons spend money, email vendors, talk to Amazon, move real stock or delete records. If you think one is needed, **ask Adam or Becca first**.
:::

## Amazon buttons (FBA Shipment → Next shipment)

![Amazon buttons in the shipment menu, all marked do not press](img/dnp-fba-menu.png)

- **Create plan**
- **Refresh options**
- **Re-check**
- **Get prices again**
- **Cancel plan**
- **Confirm freight** – costs money
- **Confirm placement** – costs money
- **Mark as sent**

Only Adam or Becca press these.

## Email PO

![Email PO button marked do not press](img/dnp-email-po.png)

It emails the PO to the vendor.

## Delete PO

![Delete PO in the menu, marked do not press](img/dnp-delete-po.png)

It is inside the **…** menu on a PO. It deletes the PO.

## Email Staged POs and Delete Staged POs

![Email and delete staged POs buttons marked do not press](img/dnp-staged-pos.png)

They are at the bottom of the Purchase Orders list. They email or delete every staged PO at once.

## Reverse this receipt

![Reverse this receipt button marked do not press](img/dnp-reverse-receipt.png)

It undoes a receipt. If a receipt is wrong, ask Becca.

## Production buttons

- **Finish Run** – moves stock and adds units to the next Amazon shipment.
- **Reverse Run** – undoes a finished run.

See [Production Run](production-run.html).

## Planning buttons (Becca only)

- **Commit PO Planning** and **Clear PO Planning Selections**
- **Commit Run Planning** and **Clear Run Planning**

## Anything else

- **Delete** on any record.
- **Create "…"** in a part list. It makes a new part.
- **New Part**.
- **Add found item** in a count.

When in doubt: **stop and ask**.
