## Approvals

**What it is for:** some stock changes are big, for example a large count difference or a disposal. They wait here for a "yes" before they change the stock.

**Who uses it:** Becca and Adam.

**What you see:** **Approvals → Ledger** lists the waiting changes. Most days it is empty.

Do not approve anything unless you are Becca or Adam.

## Flags

**What it is for:** a flag is a problem the system found. Examples: a part with no vendor, a count difference, a short Amazon shipment.

**Who uses it:** Becca. Others may be assigned a flag.

| Sub-page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **My Open Flags** | Flags assigned to you. |
| **All Open Flags** | Every open flag. |

![Open flags list](img/flags-01-all.png)

To work on a flag:

1. Click the flag.
2. Read the **Title** and **Note**.
3. Fix the cause in the right place, or ask the person for that area (see [Who to ask](who-to-ask.html)).
4. When it is fixed, set **Status** to **Resolved**.

## Returns Grading

**What it is for:** Amazon sends products back to us (removal orders). We check each item and decide what to do with it.

**Who uses it:** Sabith. He grades the returns here now.

![Returns Grading: one returned item with its grading boxes marked, removal order id blacked out](img/ret-01-grading.png)

The list shows each returned product and how many we expect. Click an item. Then:

1. Count the items. Type the number in **Received Qty** (1).
2. Choose a **grade** (2).
3. **Wrong item came back?** Choose the grade **Wrong item received**. In **What came back** (3), write what Amazon sent instead: the name, the brand, and the barcode if you can see it. Add photos in **Damage Photos** (A).
4. Choose a **Disposition** (4): what we do with it.
5. If you keep it, choose the **Putaway Bin** (5) and the **Expiration Date**.

Before December 1, grading does not change stock. Real stock moves stay in Fishbowl.
