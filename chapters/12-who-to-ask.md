Look in the handbook first. Then ask the person for that area.

| Area | Ask |
|---|---|
| Warehouse: bins, counts, builds, labels, Clear Conscience (CC) orders | **Chad** |
| Payments and receiving costs | **Vicky** |
| System problems and bugs (something in RemBase does not work) | **Adam** |
| Planning decisions and Becca's approval buttons | **Becca** |
| Amazon claims | **Karly** |
| Any button on the [Do not press](do-not-press.html) list | **Adam**, before you press |
| Amazon shipments | Anyone may press the shipment buttons. Not sure? Ask **Adam** first. |

## When you ask, send

1. The page name (for example: Receiving).
2. The record name (for example: TEST-HB-1).
3. What you clicked.
4. What you expected to see.
5. What you saw instead. A screenshot helps.

## Remember

- Until December 1, real work goes in **Fishbowl**.
- Practice only with **TEST** items.
- Not sure? **Stop and ask.** It is always OK to ask.
