| Any button on the [Do not press](do-not-press.html) list | **Adam** or **Becca**, before you press |
| Money, Amazon shipments | **Adam** or **Becca** |

## When you ask, send

1. The page name (for example: Receiving).
2. The record name (for example: TEST-HB-1).
3. What you clicked.
4. What you expected to see.
5. What you saw instead. A screenshot helps.

## Remember

- Until December 1, real work goes in **Fishbowl**.
- Practice only with **TEST** items.
- Not sure? **Stop and ask.** It is always OK to ask.
