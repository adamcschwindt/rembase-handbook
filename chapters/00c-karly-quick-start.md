## Read this first

- **Claims are real work now, not practice.** You file each claim in **Amazon Seller Central**. RemBase finds the claims and prepares the text and the files.
- **RemBase saves by itself.** There is no Save button. Press **Tab** or click outside the box.
- **Made a typing mistake?** Press **Cmd+Z** (**Ctrl+Z** on Windows) right away to undo it.
- **Never delete a record.** If something gets deleted by mistake, tell Adam.
- **Cannot open a page?** Ask Adam for access.

**When:** Tasks 1, 2 and 3 every Monday. Tasks 4 and 5 twice a week (for example Monday and Thursday).

## Task 1 – Monday: file the claims

**Goal:** file every claim on the **To file** list in Amazon.

1. In the RemBase menu, click **Claims**, then **File Claims**.
   - The **To file** tab opens first. Work from the top.
2. Click the first claim to open it.
3. Read **How To File**. Its steps are different for each type of claim. Follow them.
4. Click **Open in Seller Central**. Amazon's page opens.
5. In Amazon, search for the number in **Search For**.
6. Copy the **Packet Text** and paste it into Amazon's form.
7. Look at **Evidence Status**. If Amazon asks for proof, attach the **Amazon BOL** and the **Supplier Invoice** from the claim.
   - Send the **original** invoice (the **CANDIDATE** file), never the **HIGHLIGHTED** copy.
8. Submit the claim in Amazon. Copy Amazon's case number.
9. Paste the case number into **Case ID**.
   - RemBase sets the claim to **Filed**. It leaves the **To file** list.
10. Do the next claim.
    - **Group Value** is blank? **Still file it.**
    - Look at **Earliest Deadline**. After that date, Amazon does not pay.

**Done when:** the **To file** list is empty.

More help: [Claims](claims.html).

## Task 2 – Monday: file a defective dispute

**Goal:** ask Amazon to check a unit it graded as defective.

These claims have the Group Type **Defective dispute**. File every one. There is no minimum value.

1. Open the claim. Click **Open in Seller Central**. Amazon's **Get support** page opens.
2. Click **Create new issue**. Store: **United States**. Service: **Selling on Amazon**.
3. Select **My issue is not listed**. Paste the **Packet Text** into the box.
4. Amazon shows an automatic answer ("Policy Ineligibility"). Do not stop. Click **Contact an associate**, then **Check warehouse damaged reimbursement status**.
5. Fill in **only** the FNSKU (it is in the Packet Text). Leave **Removal order ID** and **Batch ID** empty. Click **Continue**.
6. Contact method: **Email**. Click **Submit**.
7. Copy the case number into **Case ID**.

**Done when:** every **Defective dispute** claim has a **Case ID**.

More help: [Defective dispute claims](claims.html#defective-dispute-claims).

## Task 3 – Fix a case number

**Goal:** keep every **Case ID** right.

1. To find a claim you already filed: in **File Claims**, click the tab **Filed & closed**.
2. **Wrong case number?** Open the claim. Paste the right one into **Case ID**.
3. **The claim was not really filed?** Delete the **Case ID**. The claim goes back to **To file**.
4. **Notes** says **DUPLICATE CASE ID**? Each case number belongs to **one** shipment. Check which shipment the case is for.

**Done when:** each filed claim has its own case number.

More help: [Fix a case number](claims.html#fix-a-case-number).

## Task 4 – Twice a week: check listing alerts

**Goal:** see which Amazon listings have a problem now.

1. In the RemBase menu, click **Home**, then **Listings**.
2. Look at the four counters:
   - **G – Open P1 (urgent)**
   - **H – Open P2**
   - **I – Not buyable / status problems**
   - **J – Buy box problems**
3. Look at the list **Open P1 alerts (oldest first)**. Start with these.
   - P1 = it sells 10 or more a month, and it is out of stock, not active, or the buy box is suppressed while it has stock. For our own brands, also when we lost the buy box.
4. For each row, read **Brand**, **Alert Type** and **Detail**.
5. Look at **Caused by**. If it names another alert, fix that one first.
6. Click the alert to open it. Click **Amazon Link** to see the listing on Amazon.
7. Fix the problem in Seller Central. RemBase does not change Amazon.
   - Not sure how to fix it? Ask Adam.

Good to know:

- The alert closes by itself on a later daily run, after the problem is gone.
- **Overwrite** = Amazon shows something different from what we sent. On wholesale brands this is normal.
- In **Content changes, last 7 days**, **Expected** means someone planned that change.

**Done when:** you have looked at every P1 row.

More help: [Home → Listings](home.html#listings).

## Task 5 – Mark an alert you fixed

**Goal:** show the team what you did.

1. Open the alert again (**Home** → **Listings** → click the alert).
2. In **Your check**, tick **Checked**.
3. In **Note**, write what you did. Example: "Fixed the price in Seller Central."

**Done when:** every alert you fixed is ticked **Checked** with a **Note**.
