RemBase and Fishbowl until December 1

What is RemBase?

RemBase is our new inventory system. It will replace Fishbowl on December 1, 2026.

RemBase has pages for stock, purchase orders, receiving, counting, production runs and Amazon shipments.

Until December 1: Fishbowl is real, RemBase is practice

Do all real work in Fishbowl.

Use RemBase only to practice.

  • RemBase is copied from Fishbowl every night.
  • The nightly copy does not touch TEST items, so your TEST practice stays.
  • Practice on a real part is reset by the nightly copy. That is one more reason to practice only on TEST items.
  • If you are not sure where to do something, do it in Fishbowl. Then ask the person for that area (see Who to ask).

Practice only with TEST items

Each person has their own practice PO, parts and practice run:

Person Receive this PO Your parts (scan code) Build this run Count this bin
Becca TEST-PRACTICE-BECCA-2 TEST-BECCA-A (#9111#), TEST-BECCA-B (#9112#) TEST-PRACTICE-RUN-BECCA TEST-BIN (#9001#)
Chad TEST-PRACTICE-CHAD-2 TEST-CHAD-A (#9121#), TEST-CHAD-B (#9122#) TEST-PRACTICE-RUN-CHAD TEST-BIN (#9001#)
Sabith TEST-PRACTICE-SABITH-2 TEST-SABITH-A (#9131#), TEST-SABITH-B (#9132#) TEST-PRACTICE-RUN-SABITH TEST-BIN (#9001#)

Your parts start at 0. Receive them into TEST-BIN. Your first practice PO (TEST-PRACTICE-BECCA, -CHAD or -SABITH) may already be done.

Practice in this order: 1. receive your PO, 2. count TEST-BIN, 3. build your run.

Other TEST items:

Type Name
Shared parts TEST-COMP-A, TEST-COMP-B, TEST-KIT-2PK (a 2-pack made from A and B). Others practice on these too, so their stock numbers change.
Vendor TEST Vendor (practice)
Used in the pictures PO TEST-HB-1, run TEST-HB-RUN-1

Practice making a PO by adding a line to your own practice PO. If you make a brand-new PO before December 1, RemBase gives it a PRACTICE- number (for example PRACTICE-003), not a real PO number. Use the TEST Vendor and TEST parts on it.

Never practice on a real part, a real PO or a real shipment.

How to open RemBase

  1. Open the RemBase link that Becca sent you.
  2. Sign in with your work Airtable account.
  3. You see the menu on the left side.

The menu

Page What it is for
Home Reports, today’s sales, Amazon storage space, aged stock, listing alerts and other summaries. See Home.
Inventory Find a part. See how much we have and where.
Cycle Count Count the stock in a bin.
Purchase Orders Make and track orders to vendors.
Receiving See what is arriving from vendors.
Run Planning Decide what to build next.
Production Run Build kits and packs. Enter what you built.
FBA Shipment Send stock to Amazon. Print box labels.
Approvals Big stock changes that need a “yes” first.
Flags Problems the system found.
Returns Grading Check items that Amazon sent back to us.
Backorders PO lines we are still waiting for.
CC Orders Clear Conscience orders.
Claims File Amazon claims for lost or damaged units. See Claims.

The most important rules

  1. Real work goes in Fishbowl until December 1.
  2. Practice only with TEST items.
  3. Some buttons send money, emails or orders. Read Do not press before you start.
  4. Not sure? Stop and ask. See Who to ask.

About the pictures

  • Every picture comes from RemBase.
  • A red box shows what to click or type.
  • A red number is the step number.
  • A red ✕ means do not press.
  • An orange box means a real button: you may press it, but be careful.
  • An orange $ means the button costs money.
  • Black boxes hide private data such as prices, vendors, customers and Amazon numbers. You see the real data in RemBase.
